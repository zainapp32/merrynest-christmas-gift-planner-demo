# MerryNest — Christmas Gift Planner — Free Demo

A limited **free demo** of the MerryNest Christmas Gift Planner.

**Try it:** open the demo (GitHub Pages link for this repo) and explore — Home countdown and money left, Gifts (To buy → Bought → Wrapped → Given), People and budgets, Ideas, Shop list grouped by shop, Savings tree, Tasks and Settings.

**Demo limit:** the demo saves data **10 times**. After that, saving pauses and you will be invited to get the full version. Everything you already saved stays visible.

**Full version (Etsy):** unlimited saves, one single HTML file you own forever, works offline, no subscription, no account — your data never leaves your device.
